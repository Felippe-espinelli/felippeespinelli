## About Me

I am a translational behavioural neuroscientist specialising in experimental models of neuropsychiatric disorders (PTSD, OCD, stress, and compulsivity) across species[cite: 1]. 

Currently, I serve as **Chief Research Officer at Memorify** (UK) while maintaining active research as a **Virtual Visiting Researcher at the University of Cambridge**[cite: 1]. My work sits at the intersection of computational modeling, behavioral paradigms, and applied neuroscience[cite: 1].
